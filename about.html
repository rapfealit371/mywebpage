Our Story

Founded in 2020, Party Rentals & Event Services began as a small, family-owned business built on a simple vision — to make every celebration stress-free, memorable, and filled with joy. What started with a few party machines and local rentals has grown into a trusted community favorite for high-quality event equipment, creative décor, and exceptional customer service. Our team understands that planning an event can be overwhelming, so we work closely with every client to ensure each detail is handled with care and precision. From birthdays and weddings to school carnivals, church gatherings, and corporate events, we provide everything you need to bring your ideas to life. Our inventory includes snow cone, popcorn, and cotton candy machines, projectors, screens, tables, and decorations — all carefully maintained to guarantee safety and reliability. We also offer themed packages that let clients customize their celebration to match any mood or style. What truly sets us apart is our passion for people; every event is an opportunity to help families, friends, and organizations create lasting memories together. At Party Rentals & Event Services, we don’t just rent equipment — we help turn your moments into joyful experiences that you and your guests will treasure..
